Our Story

From Garage Startup to Global Telematics

What started as a frustration with disconnected fleet data became an obsession with connected vehicle intelligence.

Since 2014
10+ years of telematics

NaviFleet was founded in 2014 by two fleet dispatchers who were tired of stitching together GPS pings, spreadsheets and phone calls just to know where their trucks were. They believed fleet telematics should be one integrated system — not a patchwork of disconnected tools.

A decade later, that vision has grown into a full-stack telematics platform — from our own OBD-II and J1939 hardware to cloud software serving 42,800+ assets across 45+ countries. But the mission hasn’t changed: make fleet operations radically simpler and safer.

What We Stand For

Three Values, Zero Compromise

Every product decision, every support interaction and every hardware upgrade is guided by these principles.

Precision First

We obsess over telemetry precision and sub-second latency. Every delayed ping is an operational blind spot, so we engineer for the tightest satellite tolerances possible.

Safety Over Speed

We will never release an update that compromises driver safety or telemetry integrity. Our stack is hardened for mission-critical commercial fleets where downtime is unacceptable.

Partnership, Not Transaction

We don’t just license software — we deploy beside you, onboard your dispatch teams and scale as your fleet grows. Your long-term operational performance is our only true metric.
