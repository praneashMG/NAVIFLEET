How quickly will I get a reply?
Sales inquiries are answered within one business day — usually within a few hours. Technical support requests via live chat or the support email typically receive a first response in under 12 minutes, 24/7.

Do you offer demos before I commit?
Yes — every prospective customer gets a free 14-day trial with demo hardware shipped to their door. We’ll also schedule a live 20-minute walkthrough with a fleet telematics engineer so you can see the exact features relevant to your operation.

Can you migrate data from our current GPS provider?
Yes. Our onboarding team handles data migration from all major telematics platforms — including vehicle profiles, historical routes, geofences and driver assignments. Most migrations are completed within 5–10 business days.

Do you support custom integrations?
Absolutely. All plans include REST API and webhook access. For enterprise customers, we also offer native connectors for popular TMS, ERP and CRM platforms — and our engineering team can scope custom integration work if needed.

What regions do you support?
NaviFleet operates in 45+ countries across North America, Europe, LATAM, APAC and the Middle East. Our multi-carrier LTE SIMs automatically negotiate the strongest local network, so hardware works internationally without reconfiguration.

How do I escalate an urgent issue?
For urgent operational incidents, call our priority support line directly at +1 (800) 555-3533 — available 24/7. Enterprise customers also have a dedicated account manager who can be reached via direct line at any time.
