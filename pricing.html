Pricing FAQ

Questions About
Billing & Plans?

Everything you need to know about how NaviFleet pricing works — from billing cycles to hardware ownership and trial terms.

How is pricing calculated per vehicle?
Every plan is billed per actively tracked vehicle, per month. There are no hidden activation fees, no separate SIM charges and no long-term contracts required — you can add or remove vehicles at any time and billing adjusts on your next cycle.

Do I need to buy hardware separately?
Telematics devices are included with every plan at no extra cost on annual subscriptions. On monthly plans, a small one-time device deposit applies (refundable on return). Demo hardware for the 14-day trial is always free.

What does the 14-day free trial include?
The trial includes full access to the Professional Fleet plan, a free demo tracker shipped to your door, and onboarding help from a dedicated specialist. No credit card is required to start, and you can cancel any time during the trial with no charges.

Can I switch plans mid-subscription?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately with a prorated charge; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do you offer volume discounts for large fleets?
Absolutely. Fleets with 100+ vehicles qualify for tiered discounts on all plans, and 200+ vehicle fleets get custom enterprise pricing with dedicated support. Contact our enterprise team for a tailored quote.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, ACH bank transfers for annual plans, and purchase orders for enterprise accounts. Invoicing is available for all annual subscriptions.
