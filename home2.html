Core GPS Features

Powerful Tools, Zero Complexity

Every feature is built to answer one question fast: where is my asset, and what is it doing?

01

Live Map & Route Playback

Watch every unit move in real time on satellite or street maps. Scrub back through up to 12 months of route history with stop, idle and speed overlays — perfect for dispute resolution and driver coaching.

02

Smart Geofence Alerts

Draw unlimited polygon or circular zones on the map. Get instant push, SMS or email alerts the moment any asset enters or leaves — including after-hours movement that shouldn’t be happening.

03

Driver Behavior Scoring

Speeding, harsh braking, rapid acceleration and cornering are logged automatically into a 100-point safety score for every driver. Identify coaching opportunities before they turn into claims.

04

Reports & Analytics

Automated daily, weekly and monthly reports for distance, idle time, fuel and safety — exportable to CSV or PDF and delivered straight to your inbox.
